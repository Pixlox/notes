### Comparing types
---

| Accommodation type | Description                                                                    | Advantages                                                     | Disadvantages                                               | Suitable for who                                                                     |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Airbnb             | Rental properties offered by hosts, ranging from single rooms to entire homes. | Affordable for groups.   Unique, local experiences.            | Quality can vary. <br> May lack professional service.       | Travelers seeking personalized experiences and families/groups.                      |
| Hostel             | Budget-oriented accommodations with shared dormitories and common areas.       | Very cheap. <br> Great for meeting other travellers.           | Limited privacy. <br>May not suit those who prefer comfort. | Backpackers, solo travelers, and budget-conscious individuals.                       |
| Expensive hotel    | High-end accommodations with luxury amenities and professional services.       | Comfortable and luxurious. <br>Reliable, high-quality service. | Very costly. <br>May lack local 'experience'.               | Business travelers, couples on a luxury vacation, and those seeking premium comfort. |
