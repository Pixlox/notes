1. A body will remain at rest or in uniform motion in a straight line unless it is compelled to change this state by forces impressed upon it.
2. A body acted upon by an external unbalanced force will accelerate in proportion to the magnitude of this force in the direction in which this force acts.
3. To every action (or force) there is an equal and opposite reaction (or force).

- Statics - things that don't move
- Dynamics - things that move
- Kinematics - study of motion through analysis of displacement, velocity and acceleration
