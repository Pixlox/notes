---
- Our solar system consists of Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune
- Our sun is in the centre, and every planet revolves around it.
- We are part of the Milky Way galaxy, and there are multiple other galaxies.
- Our sun is a star; there are many other stars.
- The Big Bang is our best theory on how the universe was created.
